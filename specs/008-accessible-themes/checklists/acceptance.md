# Acceptance Checklist

- [ ] Blue, red, green and orange are named free themes.
- [ ] Premium is discoverable and fail-closed without entitlement.
- [ ] Current selection is announced and not color-only.
- [ ] Token contrast gates pass.
- [ ] Keyboard activation and focus visibility are proven automatically.
- [ ] Reduced-motion avoids view-transition animation.
- [ ] 320 CSS px and 400% zoom are manually reviewed or explicitly blocked.
- [ ] Screen-reader behavior is manually reviewed or explicitly blocked.
- [ ] No authorization or political meaning is derived from theme.
- [ ] No external effect, billing or storage is introduced.
