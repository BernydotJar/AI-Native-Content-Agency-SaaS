# Acceptance checklist

- [ ] Publication intent persists before the provider request.
- [ ] One fenced executor may publish.
- [ ] Compatible replay never creates a second post.
- [ ] Unknown outcome blocks automatic retry.
- [ ] Disconnect and Greenlight revocation invalidate unused authority.
- [ ] Instagram cannot publish without supported reachable media.
- [ ] Tokens, provider bodies and post content stay out of logs/audit.
- [ ] CI makes zero real X or Meta publication requests.
- [ ] Authorized sandbox receipts are reconciled before production review.
